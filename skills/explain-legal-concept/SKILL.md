**Provide a clear, practical explanation following these guidelines:**

### 1. Simple Definition
Explain in 1-2 sentences of plain English, no legal jargon:

Use: "This means that..."

**Output Guidelines:**
- Use plain English, avoid legal jargon
- Provide concrete examples, not abstract theory
- Focus on practical application, not academic explanation
- Tailor to provided context (role, industry, purpose)
- Keep explanation concise (aim for 1-2 pages)
- Use analogies if helpful for complex concepts

- Connect to business impact, not just legal significance
- If concept is jurisdiction-specific, note differences
- Make it actionable: help them use this knowledge
