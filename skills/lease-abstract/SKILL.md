## What You'll Receive

A concise lease abstract including:

1. **Executive Summary** (one paragraph): Parties, property, term, rent, key dates
2. **Key Terms Summary**: Financial terms, critical dates, renewal/termination options
3. **Important Obligations**: Maintenance, insurance, operational requirements
4. **Notable or Unusual Provisions**: Non-standard terms or concerns
5. **Action Items**: Critical deadlines and recommendations

## Analysis Instructions

**Provide a structured lease abstract focusing on actionable information:**

### 1. Executive Summary
One paragraph covering: landlord and tenant, property address and type, lease term (start/end dates), base rent (monthly/annual), any rent escalations, and key dates to remember.

### 2. Basic Lease Information
Present in simple table format:
- Lease execution date
- Parties (full legal names)
- Premises address and square footage (rentable and usable)
- Lease term: Commencement date → Expiration date
- Rent commencement date (if different)

### 3. Financial Terms Summary
**Focus on these if selected as priority** in table format:

| Term | Details |
|------|---------|
| **Base Rent** | Monthly and annual amounts |
| **Rent Escalations** | Annual increases (%, CPI, or fixed) |
| **Additional Rent** | CAM charges, operating expenses, taxes |
| **Security Deposit** | Amount and return conditions |
| **Rent Abatement** | Free rent periods |
| **TI Allowance** | Tenant improvement amount |

If there's a rent schedule across multiple years, provide:

| Period | Monthly Rent | Annual Rent |
|--------|--------------|-------------|
| Year 1 | | |
| Year 2 | | |
| Continue as applicable | | |

### 4. Critical Dates Calendar
List important dates that require action:
- Renewal option exercise deadline
- Termination option deadline
- Lease expiration date
- Any other time-sensitive rights

### 5. Renewal & Termination Rights
**If selected as priority, analyze:**
- **Renewal Options**: Number of options, duration, notice required, renewal rent determination
- **Early Termination Right**: Termination date, notice required, termination fee, conditions
- **Expansion/Contraction Rights**: ROFO, ROFR, expansion options

### 6. Assignment & Subletting
**If flexibility is a priority:**
- Can tenant assign or sublet?
- Landlord consent required? (Reasonable or sole discretion?)
- Recapture rights or profit sharing?
- Change of control treated as assignment?

### 7. Maintenance & Insurance
**If selected as priority:**
- Who is responsible for: structural repairs, HVAC, plumbing/electrical, interior maintenance, roof, common areas
- Insurance requirements: General liability amount, property insurance, business interruption, additional insured status

### 8. Unusual or Notable Provisions
Flag any terms that are atypical or warrant special attention:
- What's unusual
- Why it matters
- Risk level (🟢🟡🔴)

### 9. Key Takeaways & Action Items
Summarize:
1. **Top 3 Favorable Terms** for tenant (or landlord if that's our role)
2. **Top 3 Concerns** to watch or renegotiate
3. **Critical Action Items** with deadlines (e.g., "Exercise renewal option by [date]")

**Output Guidelines:**
- Focus on selected priority areas
- Use tables for dense information (dates, rent schedules)
- Be concise: avoid lengthy explanations
- Highlight actionable items and deadlines
- Only include sections with meaningful information
- If a category has no special provisions, state "Standard terms" in one line
