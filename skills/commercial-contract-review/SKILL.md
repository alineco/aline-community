## What You'll Receive

A focused contract analysis including:

1. **Contract Overview** (3-4 sentences): Type, parties, value, term, and purpose
2. **Risk Assessment**: Key provisions organized by selected focus areas with risk levels, contract quotes, and recommendations
3. **Unusual or Missing Provisions**: Non-standard terms or important gaps
4. **Top 3-5 Action Items**: Most critical issues to address before signing
5. **Final Recommendation**: Approve / Approve with changes / Do not approve (with rationale)

---

## Analysis Instructions

**Provide a concise, actionable contract review following these guidelines:**

### 1. Contract Overview
Briefly summarize: contract type, parties, effective date, term, total value (if discernible), and what the agreement covers.

### 2. Risk Assessment
For each selected focus area, analyze relevant provisions in table format:

| Provision | Risk Level | Contract Quote | Impact & Recommendation |
|-----------|------------|----------------|-------------------------|
| [Provision name] | 🟢🟡🔴 | [Key language] | [1-2 sentence analysis] |

**Risk Level Guide:**
- 🟢 Low Risk (favorable/market-standard)
- 🟡 Moderate Risk (review recommended)
- 🔴 High Risk (must address)

Only include provisions with meaningful findings. If a focus area has no concerns, state "No significant issues identified" in one line.

### 3. Unusual or Missing Provisions
- **Unusual**: Flag any non-standard terms with explanation and risk level
- **Missing**: Identify important provisions that should be added (e.g., force majeure, assignment restrictions, warranty disclaimers)

### 4. Priority Action Items
Rank the 3-5 most critical issues to address before signing, with specific recommendations for each.

### 5. Final Recommendation
- ✅ **Approve** — Ready to sign as-is
- 🟡 **Approve with Changes** — Acceptable if noted items are addressed
- 🔴 **Do Not Approve** — Significant issues must be resolved first

Include brief rationale (2-3 sentences) and list top 3 negotiation priorities.

**Output Guidelines:**
- Be concise: 1-2 sentences per finding
- Focus on business impact, not legal theory
- Only flag provisions with actual concerns
- Prioritize actionable insights over comprehensive coverage
- Use risk indicators (🟢🟡🔴) meaningfully, not on every provision
