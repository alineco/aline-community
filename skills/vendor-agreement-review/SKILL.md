## What You'll Receive

A customer-focused vendor agreement analysis including:

1. **Agreement Overview** (2-3 sentences): Parties, services, value, term
2. **Commercial Terms Assessment**: Pricing, payment, renewal, and termination analysis with risk levels
3. **Legal Terms Assessment**: Liability, indemnification, data protection, and IP analysis
4. **Top 3-5 Negotiation Priorities**: Most important issues to address
5. **Final Recommendation**: Approve / Approve with changes / Do not approve

## Analysis Instructions

**Provide a focused vendor agreement review from our perspective as the customer:**

### 1. Agreement Overview
Briefly summarize: parties, what we're buying, contract value, term/renewal, and overall risk assessment (Low/Medium/High).

### 2. Commercial Terms Assessment
Analyze these provisions **only if relevant to selected priorities** in table format:

| Provision | Contract Terms | Risk Level | Impact & Recommendation |
|-----------|----------------|------------|-------------------------|
| Pricing & Payment | [Summary] | 🟢🟡🔴 | [Analysis] |
| Term & Renewal | [Summary] | 🟢🟡🔴 | [Analysis] |
| Termination Rights | [Summary] | 🟢🟡🔴 | [Analysis] |
| Effect of Termination | [Summary] | 🟢🟡🔴 | [Analysis] |

**What to look for:**
- Pricing: Fixed or variable? Hidden fees? Payment terms (we prefer Net 45+)?
- Term: Initial term length (we prefer ≤1 year)? Auto-renewal (we prefer NO)?
- Termination: Can we terminate for convenience? Penalties?
- Effect: Data return? Refunds? Transition assistance?

Only include provisions with actual issues or relevant to selected priorities.

### 4. Missing Provisions
Identify any important provisions that are absent and should be added.

### 5. Priority Negotiation Items
List 3-5 most critical issues ranked by importance, with proposed resolution for each.

### 6. Final Recommendation
- ✅ **Approve as-is** — Ready to sign
- 🟡 **Approve with changes** — Acceptable if noted items addressed
- 🔴 **Do not approve** — Significant issues require resolution

Include brief rationale (2-3 sentences).

**Output Guidelines:**
- Focus on customer protections and flexibility
- Only analyze provisions relevant to selected priorities
- Be concise: 1-2 sentences per finding
- Prioritize business impact
- If a section has no issues, summarize in one line
