## Analysis Instructions

**Provide a practical, actionable alert analysis following these guidelines:**

### 1. Executive Summary
In 3-4 sentences, answer:
- What is the new law/regulation/ruling/development?
- When does it take effect (key dates)?
- Who is affected (industries, geographies, company sizes)?
- Why does it matter (what's the business impact)?

### 2. What Changed
Explain the substantive change in plain language:
- What was the prior rule/approach?
- What is the new rule/requirement?
- What drove this change (legislation, court case, regulatory action)?

### 3. Applicability Assessment
**Does this apply to our company?**

Analyze based on provided company context:

**Geographic Scope**: [Where does this apply? Do we operate there?]
**Industry Scope**: [Which industries? Are we included?]
**Size Thresholds**: [Employee/revenue minimums? Do we meet them?]
**Activity Triggers**: [What activities trigger coverage? Do we do those?]

**Conclusion**:
- ✅ **Clearly Applies** — You meet the criteria [explain why]
- 🟡 **Likely Applies** — You probably meet criteria [explain and recommend confirmation]
- ⚪ **Uncertain** — Needs more analysis [explain what's unclear]
- ❌ **Does Not Apply** — You don't meet criteria [explain why not]

### 8. Next Steps Recommendation
**Provide clear recommendation:**

- ❌ **No action required** — This doesn't apply to you
- 👀 **Monitor** — May apply in future, track developments
- 💬 **Internal review** — Schedule meeting with [stakeholders] to discuss
- 🚀 **Begin compliance project** — Start implementing action items
- 👨‍⚖️ **Engage outside counsel** — Complex, needs legal expertise

Include 2-3 sentence rationale for recommendation.

**Output Guidelines:**
- Write in plain language, avoid legalese
- Focus on practical business impact, not legal theory
- Be specific about applicability to provided company context
- Prioritize actions by urgency and impact
- If alert doesn't apply, say so briefly and explain why
- If uncertain, clearly state what additional information is needed
- Provide realistic timelines based on effective dates
- Connect recommendations to actual business risk/benefit
