## Analysis Instructions

**Provide focused feedback and improved draft following these guidelines:**

### 1. Quick Assessment
Evaluate the draft on key dimensions:

**Clarity** (Is the message easy to understand?)
- Main point clear? ✅ 🟡 ❌
- Brief explanation (1 sentence)

**Structure** (Is information organized effectively?)
- Bottom line up front? ✅ 🟡 ❌
- Easy to scan? ✅ 🟡 ❌
- Brief explanation (1 sentence)

**Tone** (Appropriate for audience and purpose?)
- Professional and appropriate? ✅ 🟡 ❌
- Confident but respectful? ✅ 🟡 ❌
- Brief explanation (1 sentence)

**Call to Action** (Clear what recipient should do?)
- Clear ask with deadline? ✅ 🟡 ❌
- Brief explanation (1 sentence)

### 2. Top 3-5 Improvements Needed
Focus on the most impactful changes:

1. **[Issue]**: [Current approach] → [Suggested fix and why]
2. **[Issue]**: [Current approach] → [Suggested fix and why]
3. **[Issue]**: [Current approach] → [Suggested fix and why]
4. Continue if more issues...

**Common issues to look for:**
- Burying the main point
- Too long or rambling
- Passive voice or weak language
- Unclear ask or missing deadline
- Wrong tone for audience
- Multiple topics in one message
- Assumes context recipient doesn't have

### 3. Word Choice Improvements (if applicable)
Flag 3-5 phrases that should be changed:

| Replace | With | Why |
|---------|------|-----|
| [Wordy/weak phrase] | [Concise/strong alternative] | [Brief reason] |

Examples:
- "At this point in time" → "Now" (More concise)
- "I wanted to reach out to" → "I'm writing to" (More direct)
- "It is possible that we could" → "We could" (Stronger)

### 4. Revised Draft
Rewrite the message incorporating all recommendations:

**Subject**: [Clear, specific subject line if email]

[Rewritten message with improvements]

**Guidelines for revised draft:**
- Lead with bottom line or request
- Use short paragraphs (2-4 sentences max)
- Use bullets or numbering for multiple points
- Active voice and strong verbs
- Specific ask with deadline
- Appropriate tone for audience and purpose
- Trim unnecessary words
- One topic per message (suggest splitting if needed)

Brief before/after comparison:

**Before:**
- Word count: X
- Main point location: [Paragraph number or buried]
- Call to action: [Clear/unclear/missing]

**After:**
- Word count: X (X% reduction/increase)
- Main point location: First sentence/paragraph
- Call to action: [Specific and clear]

**Top 3 changes made:**
1. [Change and benefit]
2. [Change and benefit]
3. [Change and benefit]

**Output Guidelines:**
- Focus on high-impact improvements, not nitpicking
- Preserve the sender's voice and intent
- Match the requested tone preference
- Be specific about what to change and why
- Keep revised draft concise (remove fluff)
- If message is effective as-is, say so briefly
- Suggest subject line improvement if email
